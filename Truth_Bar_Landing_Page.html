THE STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Your questions, answered.

From your first cleanse to your everyday routine.

Is The TRUTH Bar really a cleanser?

Yes. It is a solid face and body cleanser formulated to cleanse, gently exfoliate, and condition in one step.

Will it dry out my skin?

It was formulated to cleanse without leaving skin feeling stripped. Glycerin, shea butter, and oat protein help skin feel conditioned after cleansing. Skin responds differently; start gradually and follow with moisturizer.

What if my skin is easily unsettled?

The TRUTH Bar is well known for its ability to calm irritated, sensitive skin. You may patch test before regular use. Stop if irritation occurs.

Can I use it on my face and body?

Absolutely! We recommend getting two bars: keep one for your face and the other for your body.

What if I have acne or oily skin?

The TRUTH Bar helps remove excess surface oil and dead skin buildup. With continued use, you may notice that your excess oil becomes better controlled.

Will it shrink my pores?

Pores don’t permanently shrink. Cleansing with the TRUTH Bar helps remove oil and buildup that can make your pores appear larger and congested, while leaving your skin with a more visibly refined look.

What if I have eczema or psoriasis?

Our clients with eczema and psoriasis generally praise the TRUTH Bar for its healing properties and gentle exfoliation.

Is the TRUTH Bar an exfoliant?

Yes, the TRUTH Bar is an exfoliant. Don’t let the creamy bubbles fool you! Your skin will be appropriately polished. Be careful when using more than one exfoliator on your skin.

Will I still need moisturizer?

Yes. The conditioning base supports a comfortable cleanse. Follow with moisturizer to help maintain hydration.
